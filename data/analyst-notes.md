# Arbitrum Ecosystem Atlas, analyst notes (draft for owner review, Nov 17 2026)

Every figure below comes from data/arbitrum-ecosystem-2026-11-17.csv (30-day window to Nov 17 2026, from DefiLlama) or data/arbitrum-funding-rounds-2026-11-17.csv. The Overview reads the numbered list under "Ecosystem insights".

## Ecosystem insights

1. Natives earn more than they trade. Arbitrum-native projects are 162 of 992 rows, about 16%, and hold about 16% of summed TVL, 22% of 30-day fees and 22% of revenue, but only about 2% of DEX volume. USD.AI ($2.67M) and GMX ($1.35M) lead native fees.
2. One DEX carries the volume. Uniswap did $3.97B of the $5.98B of 30-day DEX volume, about two thirds, with $2.56M of fees. Aerodrome's Slipstream deployment did $563M, Fluid $418M and Metric $393M on under $0.4M of TVL. Camelot is the largest native DEX at $68M.
3. Lending is the largest pool of capital. Aave holds $412M of TVL on Arbitrum and earned $1.03M of fees in 30 days. Morpho holds $194M, Fluid $153M and Compound $55M. Dolomite is the largest native lender at $18M.
4. GMX is still the native perps leader. GMX holds $184M of TVL and earned $1.35M of fees in 30 days. Hyperliquid ($80M), Aster ($52M) and edgeX ($46M) show larger balances than most apps, but these are bridge deposits for exchanges that run on their own chains.
5. The top fee earner is not DeFi. Aethir earned $5.5M of fees in 30 days from node licenses and rewards, the most of any project on Arbitrum. Hypernova earned $1.43M, Spiko $0.97M and Lava $0.70M.
6. Curators and exchanges sit outside TVL. Vault curators such as Steakhouse Financial and Gauntlet report $163M that already sits in Morpho, Euler and Silo, and Binance holds about $1.26B of reserves on Arbitrum. The Atlas leaves both out of TVL totals.
7. Program money is broad but small. Open House rounds name 26 teams across 30 awards totalling about $0.74M, Arbitrum Gaming Ventures backs 9 projects, and DRIP Season 1 funded looping on 6 lenders with ARB paid to users rather than to the protocols. Robinhood Chain co-funded 7 of the program awards.
8. A few large rounds dominate. Since Jan 2025, 67 Arbitrum related rounds disclosed about $188M, excluding acquisitions and Rain's corporate token treasury. Zama ($57M), Variational ($50M) and Ostium ($20M) are the largest.
9. Funding research is still thin. 554 of 992 projects have not been researched for funding yet, and 28 of the 47 native projects with a tier have no disclosed round on record.
